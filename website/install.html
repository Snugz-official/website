Add Snugz to Chrome

Takes about a minute. Works on Chrome and Edge on your laptop or desktop.

Download Snugz (.zip)
  1. Unzip the downloadRight-click the file → Extract All. You'll get a snugz-extension folder.
  2. Open Chrome's extensions pageCopy this into the address bar:
    chrome://extensions
  3. Turn on "Developer mode"The switch is in the top-right corner.
  4. Click "Load unpacked"Choose the snugz-extension folder, then pin Snugz from the puzzle-piece menu.
  5. Log in from the Snugz iconUse the same email and password as your fit profile, then open any shirt or jeans on Flipkart or Myntra and open its size chart.
